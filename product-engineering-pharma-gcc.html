Building Product & Engineering Organizations Inside Pharma GCCs

A scalable product and engineering organization inside a pharma GCC needs clear ownership, strong technical foundations, product thinking and a business-facing operating model.
By Mohammad Mujeeb Beg · Perspective

Building a product and engineering organization inside a pharmaceutical GCC is fundamentally an organization-design challenge as much as a technology challenge. The objective is to create teams that can understand business needs, make sound technology decisions and continuously improve products and platforms.

Give teams meaningful ownership

Teams perform differently when they own outcomes rather than a queue of requests. Clear product ownership creates accountability for customer needs, roadmap choices, prioritization and value.

Pair product and engineering

Product managers and engineering leaders should operate as partners. Product brings the customer and business context; engineering brings technical depth, architecture and delivery discipline. Together they create a sustainable product roadmap.

Create engineering standards

Scale requires common practices for architecture, security, quality, observability, testing, DevOps and data. Standards should enable teams rather than become bureaucracy.

Build leadership depth

As organizations grow, leadership cannot depend on one person. Managers, product leaders, architects and technical leads need clear decision rights and development paths.

“Scale is not the number of engineers. Scale is the organization’s ability to repeatedly create valuable products.”

For pharma GCCs, the opportunity is to build engineering organizations that are deeply connected to the enterprise while retaining the technical excellence and product discipline needed to move quickly.
